I enjoy writing. I am a better engineer and thinker when I am forced to put my thoughts down in writing. I find it too easy to hold thoughts that seem well-formed and logical in my head but fall apart the second I try to communicate them. 

Another reason I believe I should write more is that AI is starting to erode my daily deep thinking. In the past, I would regularly spend hours every day in deep thought, involving anything from coding to writing emails to researching some niche question that had come up. These opportunities to exercise my brain have vanished in recent months. I value having a functioning brain, and I would not like it to waste away, so I will write.

I'd like to write with some regularity, because otherwise I won't write at all. I don't really know yet whether I want people to read my writing - on one hand, the writing will be bad for quite a while, but on the other hand having other people read this stuff will incentivize me to get better faster. Either way, nobody knows about this blog yet, and it could remain that way indefinitely.
