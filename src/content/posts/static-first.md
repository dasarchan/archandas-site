---
title: "Static First"
description: "Why this site starts with simple static files instead of a server."
pubDate: 2026-07-19
tags: ["web", "hosting"]
---

For a personal site, static files are hard to beat.

They are cheap to host, fast to load, easy to back up, and wonderfully boring in
production. There is no database to patch, no app server to restart, and no monthly bill
unless the project grows into something that truly needs one.
